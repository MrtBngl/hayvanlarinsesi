# 🐾HayvanlarınSesi (Street Animal Rights & Information Website)

An informative web project focused on raising awareness about street animals in Türkiye, including their legal rights, protection laws, and social importance.

This website was developed to provide accessible and understandable information about animal welfare, street animal rights, and related regulations while encouraging social awareness and responsibility.

---

## 📌 Project Purpose

The main purpose of this project is to inform users about:

- Street animal rights in Türkiye
- Animal protection laws and regulations
- Social awareness regarding animal welfare
- The importance of protecting and supporting stray animals

The platform aims to present this information in a simple, modern, and user-friendly way.

---

## ✨ Features

- 📖 Informative content about street animal rights
